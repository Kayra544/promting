Prompt Engineering & AI Masterclass

Comprehensive guide covering all course materials, LLMs, chat models, and applications.

01. Introduction to Prompting

Prompt engineering is the foundation of structuring precise instructions for AI models. It bridges human intent and machine understanding to generate accurate, context-aware responses.

02. Understanding LLMs

Large Language Models use massive neural networks and transformer architectures. They process tokens and calculate probabilities to generate human-like text outputs.

03. Chat Models & Alignment

Chat models incorporate safety layers and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) to ensure helpful, safe, and coherent multi-turn interactions.

04. Designing AI Applications

Building real-world apps involves system prompts, API orchestration, handling latency, and integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for up-to-date data.

05. Advanced Prompts & Context

Mastering few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought reasoning, and constraint-based generation to tackle complex reasoning and programming tasks successfully.
